# Ranjeet Labs — Product Roadmap

## Product principles

1. Ship small, useful features.
2. Keep free tools fast and accessible.
3. Avoid paid APIs until usage justifies cost.
4. Keep every product independent where its runtime or deployment differs.
5. Never market a prototype as production-ready.
6. Protect user files and credentials.
